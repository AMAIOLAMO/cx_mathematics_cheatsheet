# CxRedix's Mathematical Cheatsheet

This cheatsheet serves as a collection of mathematical constructs
that I found useful over the course of my learning.

Most of the stuff shown here does not try to explain a certain concept
in detail. It rather tries to ***supplement*** you in remembering the most
***important aspects*** of the given mathematical construct within a certain
context.

This cheatsheet is written based on what I've learnt over the years, which may
be extremely biased towards certain aspects of mathematics, specifically:

1. Basic Algebra
2. Linear Algebra
